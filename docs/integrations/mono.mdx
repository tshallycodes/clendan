# Mono

Mono provides open banking data for African markets, covering Nigeria, Ghana, Kenya, and other markets across the continent. Clendan uses Mono to pull bank balances, transaction history, and account details from supported institutions.

## Supported Banks

Mono connects to major African banks including GTBank, Zenith, Access, First Bank, UBA, Sterling, Stanbic IBTC, GCB, Ecobank, Equity Bank, KCB, and Co-op Bank.

## Connecting

1. Navigate to **Integrations → Banking** in your dashboard
2. Select the **Africa** tab in the bank picker
3. Choose your bank and click **Connect**
4. Complete authentication via Mono Connect
5. Clendan will begin syncing your transactions automatically

## Data Synced

- Account balances (current and available)
- Transaction history
- Account metadata (account number, bank name, currency)
