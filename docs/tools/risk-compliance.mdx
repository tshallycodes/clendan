# Risk & Compliance

Risk & Compliance consolidates fraud detection, regulatory monitoring, and audit preparation into one always-on risk layer. The fraud engine scores every transaction in real-time; the compliance engine enforces AML, KYC, and GDPR rules; and the audit module maintains evidence packs and PBC lists so the organisation is always audit-ready.
