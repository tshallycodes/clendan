# AR & Collections

AR & Collections covers the full accounts receivable lifecycle — from real-time invoice monitoring and AI urgency classification, through tiered reminder cadences, to automatic legal escalation. Collections actions are gated by per-debtor communication limits and minimum balance thresholds.
