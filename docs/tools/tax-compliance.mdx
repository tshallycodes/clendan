# Tax Compliance

Tax Compliance pulls live invoice and expense data from connected accounting sources to compute net VAT position, detect missing or incorrect tax codes, and generate an AI-authored filing risk summary. Approval routing triggers automatically when the computed liability exceeds the configured threshold.
