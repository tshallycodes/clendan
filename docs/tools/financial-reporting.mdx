# Financial Reporting

Financial Reporting aggregates invoices, bills, expenses, payments, and bank account balances to produce a financial snapshot for a configurable period. It computes gross and net margin, current ratio, and period-over-period changes, then calls Claude for a CFO-level narrative and anomaly assessment. An approval record is created when anomalies are detected or financial health is assessed as `at_risk`.

## What it does

- Builds a financial snapshot: revenue, COGS, opex, gross profit, net profit, total assets, liabilities, and net assets
- Computes cash inflows and outflows from payment records within the period
- Computes gross margin %, net margin %, and current ratio
- Compares current period against the prior period of equal length and calculates % changes
- Flags period-over-period anomalies when any of revenue, expense, or net income deviates by more than `anomaly_variance_pct`
- Claude produces narrative summaries for P&L, balance sheet, and cash flow, and assigns an overall health status

## Decisions

| Decision | When |
|---|---|
| `auto_approved` | No anomalies detected and Claude assesses overall health as `strong` or `stable` |
| `approval_required` | Anomalies detected, or Claude assesses overall health as `at_risk` |

## Policy fields

| Field | Default | Description |
|---|---|---|
| `lookback_days` | `30` | Reporting period length in days |
| `anomaly_variance_pct` | `0.25` | Period-over-period deviation threshold for anomaly flag (25%) |

## Output data

Key fields in `output_data`:

- `revenue_cents` / `cogs_cents` / `opex_cents` / `gross_profit_cents` / `net_profit_cents`: P&L figures
- `total_assets_cents` / `total_liabilities_cents` / `net_assets_cents`: balance sheet figures
- `cash_inflows_cents` / `cash_outflows_cents` / `net_cash_cents`: cash flow figures
- `gross_margin_pct` / `net_margin_pct` / `current_ratio`: computed ratios
- `revenue_change_pct` / `expense_change_pct` / `net_income_change_pct`: period changes
- `health_status`: `healthy`, `watch`, `concerning`, or `critical`
- `top_recommendation`: Claude's single most actionable recommendation
- `period_anomalies`: array of period-over-period anomaly strings
