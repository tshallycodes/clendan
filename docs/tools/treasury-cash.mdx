# Treasury & Cash

Treasury & Cash aggregates multi-bank balances, generates rolling 30/60/90-day cash flow forecasts, monitors unhedged FX exposures, and tracks actuals against budget — all in one treasury layer. Runway alerts, payroll reserve enforcement, and bank counterparty limits are configurable per tenant.
